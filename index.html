Before you call

The questions everybody asks.

How much does pressure washing cost?

It depends on square footage, how bad the staining is and how easy the property is to get water and equipment to. A standard two-car driveway is a very different job from a 4,000 sq ft parking lot. Send a photo to 580-467-0087 and you'll get a flat, written number — free, with no obligation and no follow-up sales pressure.

Will pressure washing damage my roof or my siding?

It absolutely can, which is exactly why roofs and siding don't get pressure washed here — they get soft washed. That means low pressure and a cleaning solution that does the work chemically. High pressure on asphalt shingles strips the granules that protect them, and on siding it forces water behind the panels. Concrete is the surface that can take real pressure, and that's where it gets used.

Do I need to be home while you work?

No. As long as there's access to an outdoor spigot and to the areas being cleaned, most jobs are done without you there. You'll get photos when it's finished. If you'd rather walk the property together at the end, just say so when you book and we'll schedule around you.

How long does it take?

Most residential jobs are a single visit — a driveway and sidewalks is typically one to two hours, a full house wash a half day. Larger commercial lots and multi-service jobs get scheduled with a window so you're not guessing.

Will the chemicals hurt my plants or my pets?

The detergents used for soft washing are diluted and rinse away, but they still get handled carefully. Plants and shrubs are watered down before work starts and rinsed again after, and beds nearest the work get covered. Pets should stay inside while the crew is on site and until surfaces have dried.

Do you take commercial work?

Yes. Storefronts, restaurant patios, dumpster pads, drive-thru lanes, sidewalks and parking lots. Commercial jobs can be scheduled after hours or overnight so nothing interrupts your business, and recurring schedules are available at a better rate than one-off visits.

How often should exterior cleaning be done?

In Oklahoma, most homes want a house wash every 12 to 24 months and concrete every 12 to 18 — north- and shade-facing surfaces grow algae faster and may need it sooner. Roofs generally go two to four years between soft washes depending on tree cover.
